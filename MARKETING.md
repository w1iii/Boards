## 5. FAQ / Objection handling

**Q: Are these the actual exam questions?**
A: No — the actual NLE is confidential. Boards' AI engine *studies* real NLE practice questions from top Philippine review sites (like RNpedia) to match their style, format, and difficulty, then generates fresh original questions. You practice on the same *kind* of questions, not the same questions.

**Q: What makes this different from free question banks?**
A: Three things: (1) grounded in real NLE questions from top review sites, not generic content, (2) AI generates unlimited fresh questions — you never run out of drills, (3) full rationales for every choice, so you learn from wrong answers too.

**Q: How do you know it matches the actual boards?**
A: The AI's reference set is real NLE practice questions scraped from established Philippine review sites, plus the PRC's official table of specifications. It's trained to match that style and Philippine clinical context (DOH programs, PhilHealth, RA 9173).

## 6. Compliance note

Scraped questions are used as **style references only** — the AI generates original questions and does not output the scraped content verbatim. Marketing should say "studies real NLE questions from top review sites" and *not* claim ownership of RNpedia's content or imply the actual NLE questions are leaked. Do not sell or redistribute scraped questions as a standalone question bank. If scaling, consider licensing/partnership with question sources or building your own review-center question partnerships.
